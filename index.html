What We Do, Simply Put

We're the boots on the ground for smart city technology.

Cities, campuses and technology companies are installing more connected equipment every year: solar-powered trash compactors, sensors, kiosks, networks and monitoring devices. All of it has to be installed, connected, cleaned, repaired and kept running out in the field.

That's our job. You send us the work order, we send a trained, local technician to the site, get it done right, and report back with photos. You never have to hire, travel or manage a field crew yourself.

And if you run your own field team, our Superior Digitech Field Service App is your team’s command center for finding, fixing and maintaining equipment, with AI guidance and live updates at every step.
